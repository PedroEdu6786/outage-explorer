- `self._execution.reserve()` **claims the execution slot for this request**. It doesn’t read data yet. The concrete implementation is **`VerifiedLauncher.reserve()`** in [launcher.py:57](/Users/PECRUZ/Projects/outage-explorer/src/outage_explorer/infrastructure/worker_runtime/launcher.py:57). It: 1.
